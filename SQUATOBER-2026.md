## Day 2 — Friday, October 2, 2026 — Bending in a Polo

| Exercise | Scheme |
| --- | --- |
| Back squat | 4 sets of 8 reps 62% |
| Seated DB shoulder press (superset a) | 4 sets of 10 reps |
| Slow strict chin ups (superset b) | 4 sets of 3-5 reps |
| DB Bulgarian split squats (superset a) | 3 sets of 5 reps each leg |
| DB RDL's (superset b) | 3 sets of 5 reps |
| Zercher squats (cardio) | 2 sets of 10 reps |
| DB or KB farmers carry (cardio) | 4 sets of 20 yds |

Warm up with 5 jumping jacks and 5 pushups, repeating as needed. 2 min rest between sets, 90 sec between Zercher sets and 60 sec between carries; at 62% the belt gets the day off, and all 32 squat reps should look identical with depth that leaves no doubt. Recovery: extra meaty pot roast, 30 min walk, sleep 8+ hrs.
